## 2026-09-08
**Energy Dividend Plays Shine as Rates Hold Steady Near 4.8%**
With the 10-year yield anchored at 4.78% and broad equities under mild pressure, energy stocks are carving out relative strength as defensive income vehicles. Oil prices remain elevated near $100 per barrel, supporting cash generation and dividend resilience across majors and integrated players—a backdrop where rate-sensitive utilities lose appeal but high-yielding energy names attract yield-starved investors. The sector's multi-decade dividend track records (Exxon's 43-year streak, for instance) and explicit shareholder return programs are resonating as alternatives to fixed income in a higher-rate regime.
