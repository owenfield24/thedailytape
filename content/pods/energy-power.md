## 2026-09-08
**Oil majors capitalize on rising crude prices and low-cost growth opportunities**
Energy majors are navigating a favorable macro backdrop as crude prices approach $100 per barrel, prompting strategic capital deployment across the sector. Companies like ExxonMobil and Chevron are advancing high-return, low-cost projects—from Venezuela expansion plans to managing geopolitical risks like Kazakhstan's project pause—while maintaining shareholder returns through consistent dividend growth. This environment rewards operators with disciplined capital allocation and exposure to advantaged resources.

Investors should monitor crude price momentum as a key catalyst and track how majors balance growth investments with shareholder distributions. Watch for updates on international projects and any disruptions to supply, as geopolitical risks remain a pricing factor; also pay attention to whether dividend growth remains a priority as companies reinvest in their portfolios.
